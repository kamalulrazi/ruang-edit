Vague Transcode

Upload your video through TikTok Studio.

How it works
  1. Choose a video in TikTok Studio.
  2. The original file follows TikTok’s upload flow.
  3. Choose Public or Private in TikTok Studio. TikTok controls when a post becomes available.
  4. Add your caption and sound in TikTok Studio. Vague adds #transcode #transcodevague to your caption.

Your video is not sent to Vague’s server for processing.
